U-TECH Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

U-TECH Publisher is an internal tool operated by U-TECH CAD CAM Solutions, Vasai East, Palghar, Maharashtra, India. It publishes U-TECH's own social media content to U-TECH's own Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn accounts.

Who uses the app

Only U-TECH CAD CAM Solutions staff sign in to U-TECH Publisher, using U-TECH's own accounts. The app is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Video and image files that U-TECH places in its own Google Drive folder for publishing.
  • Permission to upload videos to U-TECH's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Permission to publish posts to U-TECH's own Instagram and LinkedIn accounts.

How data is used and stored

Files are downloaded only for the moment needed to upload them to the chosen platform, then deleted. Access credentials are stored encrypted and used only to publish U-TECH's content. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of visitors, viewers or any third party, and does not use Google user data for advertising.

Google API Services

U-TECH Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party access) or the equivalent Instagram and LinkedIn settings.
